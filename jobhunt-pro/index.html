About this rebuild

Independent rebuild of your Base44 "NextRole / JobHunt Pro" app. The tracker, profiles, and pipeline work exactly on-device. Differences from the original: job searches open the real job sites with your profile's keywords (no AI search engine, no AI-estimated salaries), posting import is manual instead of AI Extract, and email summaries don't exist in a standalone app. Your Base44 data seeded here: 2 search profiles, your 7 pipeline stages, and your 1 saved job.
